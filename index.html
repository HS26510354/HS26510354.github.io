About Me

Hi, I'm H.P. Ishini Uthkarsha Gunathilaka, a first-year undergraduate following a BSc (Hons) in Biotechnology at the Sri Lanka Institute of Information Technology (SLIIT). I completed my secondary education at Kuliyapitiya Central College, where a strong interest in Biology and Chemistry grew into a specific curiosity about molecular biology and its applications in scientific research.

Through my Biology and Chemistry laboratory practicals, I've prepared practical reports, recorded experimental data and started building the habits of careful, methodical lab work. I'm currently developing skills in micropipetting, spectrophotometry, titration and scientific report writing — and I'm looking forward to deepening all of it as my degree progresses.
